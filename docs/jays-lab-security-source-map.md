# Jay's Lab security source map

**As of:** September 23, 2026

**Use:** Public-source research index for Jay's Lab Learning Notes in active implementation about security-minded system design for AI-agent vertical apps. This is a reading and design map, not a finished publication or an assessment of any particular product.

## Read the three obligations separately

| Lens | Applicability and primary starting point |
| --- | --- |
| **SOC 2 Type II** | An AICPA attestation examination and report about a **defined service-organization system** and selected Trust Services Criteria: security, availability, processing integrity, confidentiality, and privacy. The [Trust Services Criteria](https://www.aicpa-cima.com/resources/download/2017-trust-services-criteria-with-revised-points-of-focus-2022) evaluate controls; the [description criteria](https://www.aicpa-cima.com/resources/download/get-description-criteria-for-your-organizations-soc-2-r-report) structure management's system description; the [AICPA SOC 2 guide](https://www.aicpa-cima.com/cpe-learning/publication/soc-2-reporting-on-an-examination-of-controls-at-a-service-organization-relevant-to-security-availability-processing-integrity-confidentiality-or-privacy) explains the examination. A SOC 2 report is scoped assurance, not a substitute for the separate GDPR or HIPAA duties described below. [AICPA SOC 2 guide](https://www.aicpa-cima.com/cpe-learning/publication/soc-2-reporting-on-an-examination-of-controls-at-a-service-organization-relevant-to-security-availability-processing-integrity-confidentiality-or-privacy) · [EDPB compliance guide](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/sme/be-compliant/be-compliant_en) · [HHS Security Rule](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/security/laws-regulations/index.html) |
| **GDPR** | Applies to relevant personal-data processing in the context of an EU establishment or when a non-EU organization targets people in the EU with goods/services or monitors their behavior there; assess each processing activity. [EDPB territorial-scope guidelines, Article 3](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/sites/default/files/files/file1/edpb_guidelines_3_2018_territorial_scope_after_public_consultation_en_1.pdf) Identify the **controller**, **processor**, and **subprocessors**; processor contracts and subprocessor authorization follow those roles. [EDPB controller/processor guide](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/sme/learn-the-basics/data-controller-or-data-processor_en) |
| **HIPAA/HITECH** | Applies to covered entities and their business associates, including PHI-handling subcontractors; the Security Rule protects **electronic PHI**. A health theme alone does not make an independent consumer app a covered entity or business associate. HHS specifically lists a third-party AI chatbot serving a provider portal with patient PHI as a business-associate example. [HHS Security Rule](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/security/laws-regulations/index.html) · [HHS business associates](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/privacy/guidance/business-associates/index.html) · [HHS health-app scenarios](https://www.hhs.gov/sites/default/files/ocr-health-app-developer-scenarios-2-2016.pdf) |

**Research boundary:** The sources in the next column state legal requirements, audit criteria, or published security guidance. The architecture column is our proposed implementation interpretation. GDPR/HIPAA obligations depend on the app's actual roles, data, uses, users, contracts, and jurisdictions; SOC 2 scope depends on the system and criteria named in an examination. [EDPB territorial-scope guidelines](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/sites/default/files/files/file1/edpb_guidelines_3_2018_territorial_scope_after_public_consultation_en_1.pdf) · [HHS business associates](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/privacy/guidance/business-associates/index.html) · [AICPA SOC 2 guide](https://www.aicpa-cima.com/cpe-learning/publication/soc-2-reporting-on-an-examination-of-controls-at-a-service-organization-relevant-to-security-availability-processing-integrity-confidentiality-or-privacy)

## Full-stack control-to-source map

| Boundary | Source-backed requirement or risk | Architecture inference to test in the Learning Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Browser and product UI** | GDPR requires transparent information and procedures for access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection, and applicable automated-decision rights; some rights have conditions and exceptions. [EDPB rights guide](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/sme/be-compliant/respect-individuals-rights_en) HHS warns that tracking technology can disclose PHI in regulated health apps and patient portals. [HHS tracking guidance](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/privacy/guidance/hipaa-online-tracking/index.html) OWASP documents XSS and session risks, including sensitive data exposure through browser state. [OWASP XSS](https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/cheatsheets/Cross_Site_Scripting_Prevention_Cheat_Sheet.html) · [OWASP sessions](https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/cheatsheets/Session_Management_Cheat_Sheet.html) | Show what data the agent uses, its purpose, recipients, and retention in the workflow; provide a rights-request route; prevent unsafe rendering; protect session state; review analytics and error-reporting payloads before adding third-party browser scripts. |
| **API, identity, and authorization** | HIPAA's technical safeguards include access control, audit controls, integrity, authentication, and transmission security; GDPR requires risk-appropriate technical and organizational protection. [HHS Security Rule](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/security/laws-regulations/index.html) · [EDPB controller/processor guide](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/sme/learn-the-basics/data-controller-or-data-processor_en) OWASP identifies broken object-level authorization as a major API risk. [OWASP API1](https://api-security.owasp.org/editions/2023/en/0xa1-broken-object-level-authorization/) | Treat the API as the authority for tenant, user, role, object, and purpose checks. Recheck authorization on every retrieval and tool call; never treat model output as permission. |
| **Data intake, storage, and lifecycle** | GDPR requires lawful purpose, minimization, retention limits, privacy by design/default, records of processing, and a DPIA for likely high-risk processing, including specified large-scale sensitive-data and consequential automated-evaluation cases. Health data also needs an Article 9 condition in addition to an Article 6 basis. [EDPB lawful-processing guide](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/sme/be-compliant/process-personal-data-lawfully_en) · [EDPB design, records, and DPIA guide](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/sme/be-compliant/be-compliant_en) HIPAA's Privacy Rule limits PHI uses/disclosures and applies minimum necessary where applicable; its de-identification methods are Expert Determination and Safe Harbor. [HHS Privacy Rule](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/privacy/laws-regulations/index.html) · [HHS de-identification guidance](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/special-topics/de-identification/index.html) | Label data by tenant, sensitivity, source, and permitted purpose at intake. Define deletion/export coverage for primary DB, files, vector index, prompts, traces, and backups; use synthetic data in the public example. |
| **Agent runtime and retrieval** | NIST documents direct and indirect prompt injection; OWASP documents prompt injection, excessive agent permissions/autonomy, and cross-context leakage in vector retrieval. These are security guidance, not new GDPR or HIPAA clauses. [NIST GenAI Profile](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/ai/NIST.AI.600-1.pdf) · [OWASP agentic risks 2026](https://genai.owasp.org/resource/owasp-top-10-for-agentic-applications-for-2026/) · [OWASP prompt injection](https://genai.owasp.org/llmrisk/llm01-prompt-injection/) · [OWASP excessive agency](https://genai.owasp.org/llmrisk/llm062025-excessive-agency/) · [OWASP vector weaknesses](https://genai.owasp.org/llmrisk/llm082025-vector-and-embedding-weaknesses/) | Treat retrieved text and tool results as untrusted data. Isolate tenants in retrieval, expose only task-specific tools, constrain privileges, validate structured outputs, and require human approval for high-impact writes or clinical conclusions. |
| **Model, cloud, and other vendors** | GDPR controller–processor contracts specify instructions, confidentiality, security, subprocessor authorization, assistance with rights and breaches, return/deletion, and audit support; cross-border transfers require a Chapter V mechanism such as adequacy or appropriate safeguards. [EDPB controller/processor guide](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/sme/learn-the-basics/data-controller-or-data-processor_en) · [EDPB transfers overview](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/topics/international-transfers-and-international-cooperation_en) A cloud provider that creates, receives, maintains, or transmits ePHI on behalf of a regulated entity is a business associate even if it holds encrypted data without the key; a compliant BAA is required. [HHS cloud guidance](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/special-topics/health-information-technology/cloud-computing/index.html) | Inventory every processor/subprocessor and each data hop, including model API, observability, queue, storage, and support tooling. Gate PHI flows on executed BAAs and allowed uses; gate EU personal-data flows on DPA, transfer, and purpose decisions. |
| **Operations, evidence, and recovery** | HIPAA requires risk analysis, security management, access review, incident procedures, contingency planning, periodic evaluation, and written documentation; SOC 2 examinations evaluate the design and effectiveness of scoped controls. [HHS Security Rule](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/security/laws-regulations/index.html) · [AICPA SOC 2 guide](https://www.aicpa-cima.com/cpe-learning/publication/soc-2-reporting-on-an-examination-of-controls-at-a-service-organization-relevant-to-security-availability-processing-integrity-confidentiality-or-privacy) NIST SP 800-66 Rev. 2 maps HIPAA Security Rule provisions to practical cybersecurity resources. [NIST HIPAA resource guide](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/66/r2/final) NIST's SSDF and OWASP logging guidance cover secure development and sensitive-log handling. [NIST SSDF](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/218/final) · [OWASP logging](https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/cheatsheets/Logging_Cheat_Sheet.html) | Keep dated evidence of access reviews, change approvals, vendor reviews, restore tests, incident exercises, and agent-policy changes across the intended Type II reporting period. Keep PHI out of routine evidence exports unless necessary. |
| **Breach workflow** | GDPR controllers document breaches and notify the authority within 72 hours when required; processors notify controllers without undue delay. HIPAA notice after a breach of unsecured PHI is without unreasonable delay and has a 60-day outer limit for specified notices; BA-to-CE notification also has a 60-day outer limit. [EDPB breach guide](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/sme/assess-the-risks/data-breaches_en) · [HHS Breach Notification Rule](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/breach-notification/index.html) | Build one incident timeline with discovery, classification, affected tenants/data, containment, decision owners, vendor escalation, and notification clocks. Contractual reporting windows may need to be shorter than legal outer limits. |

## AI-agent-specific reading order

1. **Threat boundary:** [NIST Generative AI Profile](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/ai/NIST.AI.600-1.pdf) identifies GenAI risk and indirect prompt injection; [OWASP LLM01](https://genai.owasp.org/llmrisk/llm01-prompt-injection/) shows how untrusted documents and tool inputs can redirect an agent.
2. **Authority boundary:** [OWASP LLM06](https://genai.owasp.org/llmrisk/llm062025-excessive-agency/) recommends limiting tools and permissions, executing in the user's context, mediating downstream requests, and requiring approval for high-impact actions.
3. **Privacy boundary:** [OWASP LLM02](https://genai.owasp.org/llmrisk/llm022025-sensitive-information-disclosure/) and [LLM08](https://genai.owasp.org/llmrisk/llm082025-vector-and-embedding-weaknesses/) cover sensitive output and retrieval leakage; the [EDPB AI-model opinion](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/news/edpb-opinion-on-ai-models-gdpr-principles-support-responsible-ai_en) treats model anonymity and lawful basis for development/deployment as case-specific questions.
4. **Regulated vendor boundary:** [HHS business-associate guidance](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/privacy/guidance/business-associates/index.html) explicitly includes a PHI-handling AI chatbot on a provider portal; [HHS cloud guidance](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/special-topics/health-information-technology/cloud-computing/index.html) explains BAA and shared-responsibility questions. A BAA defines permitted uses; it does not itself authorize unrelated model training. [HHS business-associate guidance](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/privacy/guidance/business-associates/index.html)
5. **Audit boundary:** AICPA published [TQA Section 9561 on AI's effect on SOC examinations](https://www.aicpa-cima.com/resources/download/tqa-section-9561-soc-examinations-effect-of-the-service-organizations-use-of-ai-on-soc1-and-soc2-examinations) on September 11, 2026; its landing page is public, while the full PDF requires a free account. [AICPA AI TQA landing page](https://www.aicpa-cima.com/resources/download/tqa-section-9561-soc-examinations-effect-of-the-service-organizations-use-of-ai-on-soc1-and-soc2-examinations)

## Current-rule and terminology checks

- HHS's December 2024 proposal to strengthen the HIPAA Security Rule remains a **proposed rule** on the HHS source page, which states that the current Security Rule remains in effect. Do not teach proposed safeguards as presently mandatory without a later status check. [HHS proposed-rule page](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/security/hipaa-security-rule-nprm/index.html)
- HIPAA encryption is an **addressable implementation specification**, not an optional standard: make a risk-based determination, document it, and use an appropriate alternative when warranted. [HHS encryption FAQ](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/faq/is-the-use-of-encryption-mandatory-in-the-security-rule/index.html)
- HHS does not require or recognize private **HIPAA certification** as satisfying the Security Rule. [HHS certification FAQ](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/faq/are-we-required-to-certify-our-organizations-compliance-with-the-standards/index.html)
- HIPAA's six-year documentation-retention rule should not be turned into a claim that HIPAA requires six years of patient-record retention; HHS says the Privacy Rule does not prescribe a medical-record retention period. [HHS Security Rule](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/security/laws-regulations/index.html) · [HHS medical-record-retention FAQ](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/faq/does-hipaa-require-covered-entities-to-keep-medical-records-for-any-period/index.html)
